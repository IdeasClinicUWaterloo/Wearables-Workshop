# IDEAS Clinic Sewing Machine Tutorial 

The IDEAS Clinic uses Singer Heavy Duty Sewing Machines pictured below: 

## Parts of the Sewing Machine 
See the labeled diagram and part descriptions below: 

- **Presser Foot:** Keeps the fabric pressed down to prevent bunching and ensures a smooth stitch.
- **Presser Foot Lifter:** A lever to raise and lower the presser foot.
- **Feed Dogs:** What pulls the fabric along as it is stitched.
- **Needle:** What goes through the fabric and creates the stitches.
- **Balance Wheel:** A wheel which you turn towards yourself to do manual stitching.
- **Reverse Lever:** Press this down to have the machine stitch in reverse and backstitch.
- **Bobin Compartment:** Houses the bobbin.
- **Bobbin:** Smaller spool which threads the bottom stitch.
- **Stitch Width Regulator:** Turn the dial to control the width of the stitch.
- **Stitch Length Regulator:** Turn the dial to control the length of the stitch.
- **Stitch Selection:** Turn the dial to select the type of stitch.
- **Spool Pin:** What the spool sits on. This spool threads the bottom stitch.
- **Thread Guide:** Guides the thread to be tightly wound.
- **Tension Regulator:** Turn the dial to control the tension of the thread. These machines are kept at 4. 
- **Bobbin Winder:** What the bobbin sits on when it is winded.
- **Accessory Box:** Houses various sewing machine accessories. 
- **Foot Pedal:** Moves the needle up and down to create stitches at an automated pace. The harder you press down the faster it will god. 

## How to Thread and Insert a Bobbin 
### Threading a Bobbin 
1. Select an empty bobbin and place it on the bobbin winder and slide it into place.
2. Place a thread spool on the spool pin and wind the thread following the guide on the machine.
3. Take the end of the thread and guide it through the upper hole of the bobbin. Hold the end of the bobbin as it is through the hole about 2-3 cm out from the hole.
4. As you hold on to the end of the thread, lightly press on the foot pedal for a few seconds. Once there is a initial layer of thread on the bobbin you can snip the thread that you have been holding on to.
5. Continue to press the foot pedal on a slow-medium speed and stop when the bobbin is filled about halfway looking down on it.
6. Cut the thread that is feeding into the bobbin.
7. Slide out the bobbin and take it off the bobbin winder and take the end of the thread and wrap it around and have the end stick out a bit to indicate the start of the thread.

### Inserting a Bobbin 
There are two different bobbin compartments and each machine has one of the two types. Older machines tend to have the front-loading system while newer ones have the top-loading system. The IDEAS Clinic machines have the top-loading system. Below are steps on how to insert a bobbin. 
1. Looking down at the machine, make sure that the presser foot is raised look for the a clear plastic cover - this is the bobbin compartment. There is a little black sliding button to the right of the plastic. Slide it to the right and have the plastic cover pop out and remove it from place.
2. Take out the start of the thread from the bobbin and hold it as you look at the flat side of the bobbin and orient it so that the thread is going counterclockwise.
3. Place the bobbin with the counterclockwise oreintation in its slot and hook it around the little slit that is indicated on the plastic cover.
4. Pull about 10 cm of thread out from the bobbin and place on the back of the machine. Also pull the top thread with the bobbin thread out towards the back of the machine and have a decent allowance of thread.
5. Place the plastic cover back to close the bobbin compartment.

## How to Thread a Machine
1. Take a spool of thread and assess if it is a larger or smaller spool. If it is a larger spool, from the accessory box, take out a small rod which you can stick into the stop of the machine in a slot matching the bottom of the rod. You can place the larger spool on that. If you have a smaller spool place the spool horizontally on the rod on top of the machine and place the spool stopper on the rod against the spool to prevent it from sliding around.
2. Follow the threading guide instructions along the machine.
3. Once you are by the needle, thread it and pull the thread out the back of the machine leaving a decent allowance of thread. 

## How to Manually Stitch 
Once you have threaded the machine and inserted the bobbin and have a decent allowance of both threads at the back of the machine, you are ready to start sewing!
1. Place your fabric on the feed dogs and under the presser foot making sure that the feed dogs would have enough purchase on the fabric so it can be pulled along.
2. Lower the presser foot lifter.
3. Now that your piece is secured, you can begin to hand sew by turning the balance wheel towards yourself. This will move the needle up and down and the feed dogs will pull the fabric along. You can use this method when you are starting a stitch or are at any point when you want to have slow stitches. 
 
## How to Automatically Stitch 
1. Place your fabric on the feed dogs and under the presser foot making sure that the feed dogs would have enough purchase on the fabric so it can be pulled along.
2. Lower the presser foot lifter.
3. Now that your piece is secured, you can use your hands to keep a guiding hand on the fabric and lightly press on the foot pedal. Continue to press on the foot pedal for the duration of the stitch. The harder you press on the pedal the faster it will go. 

## How to Backstitch 
At the beginning and end of your stitch, you can backstitch to go over your stitches and make them stronger so they are less likely to come apart. 
1. At the beginning of your stitch, go forward about 3-5 stitches and then while lightly pressing on the foot pedal, press and hold down the reverse lever to go back to the beginning of your sewing.
2. Once you are done backstitching, simply release the reverse lever and allow the machine to continue sewing straight.
3. At the end of your stitch, stitch forward close to the end of your piece then press and hold down the reverse lever to go back 3-5 stitches while lightly pressing down the foot pedal.
4. Once you are done backstitching, simply release the reverse lever and allow the machine to continue sewing straight.

## Finishing Sewing 
Once you have finished sewing, follow these steps to ensure that you do not damage or un-thread the needle. 
1. Before you touch your piece, make sure the needle is at its highest point and out of the fabric. You can do this by turning the balance wheel towards you and watching the top of the machine and seeing part of the thread guide sticking up at the top.
2. Once the thread guide is visible, you can raise the presser foot lifter to unclamp your fabric.
3. Then hold your fabric (easier to do so in your left hand) and gently pull it out to the back-left corner and make sure there is ample top and bottom thread sticking out from the back of the machine.
4. Once you have a decent amount of thread coming from the machine to your piece, you can use fabric scissors or thread snips to snip the thread about 1-3 cm from your piece (you can trim closer once your piece is released).
5. Now you have your sewed piece and your machine is ready to sew again!
