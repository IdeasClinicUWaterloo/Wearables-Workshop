# 4. Threading the Machine

Before you start sewing, you must make sure your sewing machine is threaded. First, ensure the presser foot is raised and the needle is in its highest position. This allows the thread to pass correctly through the machine's tension discs.

> **Note:** Threading paths vary slightly between sewing machines. Follow along with a station helper or refer to your machine's threading diagram while completing this step. The video below demonstrates the complete threading process for the machine used in this workshop.
>
> 📺 [Watch: Threading the sewing machine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q8xr-iW54Ls)

1. Place the spool of thread onto the spool pin and secure it with the spool cap.
2. Follow the numbered thread guides on the machine, making sure the thread passes through the tension discs and the take-up lever.
3. Thread the needle from front to back.
4. Insert a wound bobbin into the bobbin compartment following the machine's markings.
5. Hold the upper thread and rotate the handwheel toward you one full turn to bring the bobbin thread to the surface.
6. Pull both the upper and bobbin threads underneath the presser foot and toward the back of the machine, leaving approximately 10–15 cm (4–6 in) of thread before beginning to sew.
