# Step 4 - Assembly

With all of the pieces prepared, the glasses case can now be assembled. Before applying any adhesive, perform a dry fit to ensure the template folds correctly around the glasses and all components are positioned properly.

**Safety:** Hot glue guns and freshly applied glue can cause burns. Apply a small amount of glue at a time and avoid touching it until it has cooled.

### 1. Dry fit the case
Fold the main body around the glasses and check that the case closes comfortably.

### 2. Attach the retaining strap
Position the retaining strap across the centre of the case and use a hot glue gun to secure one end to the back of the main body. Hold it in place for a few seconds until the glue has set.

  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8fa245f9-db0d-4459-9f1e-7503bd625dc0" alt="Attaching the retaining strap" width="60%" />
</div>

### 3. Attach the hanging strap
Use a hot glue gun to attach the hanging strap to the free end of the retaining strap.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74545a17-bd6e-44fb-bd66-1d364d1c1da6" alt="Attaching the hanging strap" width="60%" />
</div>

### 4. Cut the Velcro
Cut several pieces of Velcro that are slightly larger than needed. The Velcro will be trimmed to the final size after it has been positioned on the case.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc12ba0c-f830-48b1-ae3a-8ea166859a6e" alt="Cutting Velcro pieces" width="50%" />
</div>

### 5. Secure the main body closure
Attach one pair of Velcro to the center of the main body, between the front and back halves of the case, to prevent the case from opening accidentally.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e6bc9625-ea8c-4465-a118-a33a704057fb" alt="Velcro on the main body closure" width="50%" />
</div>

### 6. Add Velcro to the retaining strap
Attach one piece of Velcro to the underside of the free end of the retaining strap and the matching piece to the front tab of the case.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/93d88477-5805-4f74-91b5-57ea36a69fa6" alt="Velcro on the retaining strap" width="40%" />
</div>

### 7. Secure the retaining strap
Fold the retaining strap over the glasses and press the Velcro pieces together to ensure they are properly aligned.

### 8. Form the hanging loop
Attach one pair of Velcro to the overlapping ends of the hanging strap to form an adjustable loop for attaching the case to a bag or backpack.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30972eed-be73-4da2-9731-106dc02cce7f" alt="Forming the hanging strap loop" width="50%" />
</div>

### 9. Final fit check
Perform a final fit check to make sure the glasses are held securely and the case opens and closes smoothly.
